## Why we started

The Bloemerstraat is a street in Nijmegen city centre where people live, work, shop and pass
through. The street is developing into a place with a fresh, varied and alternative range of
shops and venues. At the same time, there are things everyone can see could be better:
graffiti, façades that could do with a facelift, and little greenery.

Nijmegen bloeit started from the belief that we can do something about this together, with
concrete steps rather than big words.

## A bit of history

The [bombing of 22 February 1944](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardement_op_Nijmegen_(22_februari_1944))
(in Dutch) destroyed a large part of Nijmegen city centre, including the northern part of the
Bloemerstraat. The municipality counts the street as part of its post-war reconstruction area.
As a result, owners of post-war buildings in the street can apply for a
[municipal grant for refurbishing façades](/en/projects/facade-improvement/).

## Our ambition

We want to contribute to:

- a **cleaner** street, starting with removing graffiti;
- **well-kept façades**: clean, well maintained and free of distracting additions;
- **more greenery** in and around the street;
- a **lively streetscape** that builds on the street's fresh, varied and alternative range of
  shops and venues, while preserving the character of the area.

See the [plans and projects](/en/projects/) for the status of each part.

## Where we stand now

At first, we are focusing on the Bloemerstraat and the streets that lead onto it, such as
Plein 1944, Doddendaal, Eerste Walstraat and Smetiusstraat. See also the
[frequently asked questions](/en/newsletter/#area).

The initiative is just getting started. It is currently **run by one person**. Nijmegen bloeit
is not (yet) a foundation or association, and does not speak on behalf of all residents or
businesses in the street.

First we want to find out how much interest there is. If the initiative grows, a more formal
organisation could follow, for example with membership. That is not the case yet.

## Together, and concrete

Nijmegen bloeit wants to be the place where residents, businesses and property owners find
each other. Everyone looks at the street from their own perspective. By bringing ideas and
actions together through the initiative, we can achieve more than anyone could alone. We seek
to work with the municipality and existing organisations, and we choose improvements that are
genuinely visible.

## Independent

Nijmegen bloeit is an independent initiative. It is not part of the Municipality of Nijmegen.
Where we use information from the municipality, we link to the source.
