A street with an attractive streetscape and a varied range of shops does not happen overnight.
It is the result of many small steps: well-kept façades, greenery, clean streets and businesses
that feel at home there.

This is a long-term **ambition of the neighbourhood initiative**. It is not an existing project
or plan of the municipality, and we make no statements about individual businesses or
buildings. We want to make the street more attractive together with the people who live, work
and do business there.
