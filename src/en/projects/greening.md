### Who decides what?

**What owners can do themselves**

- A green façade or living wall on their own building.
- A green roof, if the roof can carry it.
- Planters on their own property.

Some changes to a building require an environmental permit.

The municipality has a grant scheme for green roofs, green façades, vertical gardens and façade
gardens. According to the municipality, the contribution for façade gardens is higher when at
least five adjacent façade gardens are created together. That makes a joint approach through
Nijmegen bloeit attractive.

**Greenery on the pavement and in the street**

The pavement, the street and squares are public space belonging to the municipality. In
Nijmegen, guidelines apply to façade gardens on the pavement, such as a maximum depth of 45
centimetres from the façade and at least 1.20 metres of free walking space to the kerb. Tenants
ask the owner for permission. We are still finding out from the municipality whether and how
façade gardens are possible in a shopping street like the Bloemerstraat. New trees or planters
in the public space need to be agreed with the municipality.
