Graffiti is one of the most visible things that spoil the streetscape of the Bloemerstraat and
Nijmegen city centre. It is also something you can tackle relatively quickly and concretely.
That is why this is the first project of Nijmegen bloeit.

We are working with an existing municipal group that deals with graffiti. We will only report
results once they are real.

### What the municipality does

The Municipality of Nijmegen removes graffiti from buildings after a report, but only with the
owner's permission. According to the municipality, this is free for owner-occupiers; costs may
apply for businesses and organisations. You can read more about this in the
[neighbourhood guide](/en/neighbourhood-guide/#melden).
