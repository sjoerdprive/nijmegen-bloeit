## The newsletter

### What data?

For the newsletter we only process your **email address** and the data needed to send the
newsletter (such as the date you signed up and whether an email was delivered).

### Why, and on what legal basis?

We only use your email address to send you the newsletter and let you know how you can get
involved. The legal basis is your **consent**. You give it by signing up and confirming your
email address via the link you receive by email. You can withdraw your consent at any time by
unsubscribing.

### Who sends the newsletter?

We use the service **Zendlijst** (zendlijst.nl). Zendlijst stores and sends the data on our
behalf (as a processor). According to the
[privacy information from Zendlijst](https://nijmegen-bloeit.zendlijst.nl/privacy) (in Dutch):

- email addresses are stored encrypted;
- the data is processed by the service providers Vercel and Neon (in Frankfurt, Germany) and emails are sent via Resend (from Ireland). These are American companies;
- the emails contain no tracking pixels and no redirected links;
- Zendlijst only uses functional cookies (for example to keep you logged in and remember your language choice) and no analytics or tracking cookies.

### How long?

Your email address stays on the list until you unsubscribe or we remove you. Your subscription
data is then deleted. According to Zendlijst, an encrypted account remains so that you can sign
up again later; you can ask Zendlijst to delete that as well.

### Unsubscribing

Every newsletter contains an unsubscribe link. You can also unsubscribe by emailing
[info@nijmegenbloeit.online](mailto:info@nijmegenbloeit.online).

## No other data

This website has no contact form, no membership administration and no payment function. We do
not collect any personal data other than described above.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its processing, to object
and to have your data transferred. To exercise these rights, contact
[info@nijmegenbloeit.online](mailto:info@nijmegenbloeit.online). We will respond within one month.

If you disagree with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data
Protection Authority ([Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/en)).
