## What we do

We aim to meet the guidelines for digital accessibility,
[WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at level AA. Among other things, this means:

- the website can be used entirely with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus;
- text has sufficient contrast and remains readable when you zoom in up to 400 percent;
- information is never conveyed by colour alone;
- the website works on mobile, tablet and computer;
- photos have a description for people who cannot see them;
- links to other websites open in a new window; this is indicated with an arrow (↗) and a text
  for screen readers;
- the website is available in Dutch and English; use the language button at the top of every
  page to switch;
- there are no moving images, autoplaying videos or pop-ups.

We check the website with automated tests and by hand. An automated test does not find every
problem, so we may have missed something.

## Where we have less influence

- **The newsletter.** Signing up is done via the sign-up page of Zendlijst, an external service.
  We have no direct influence on the accessibility of that page.
- **Links to other websites**, such as those of the Municipality of Nijmegen. Those websites
  are the responsibility of their owners, and many are only available in Dutch.

If you run into problems there, please let us know anyway. We will look for a solution together
or pass it on.

## Noticed something? Let us know

Is there something on this website you cannot read, find or use properly? Or do you have a
suggestion to make the website more accessible? Email
<a href="mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}">{{ site.contact.email }}</a>, in English or Dutch.
If possible, mention which page it concerns and what went wrong. We take every report seriously
and will respond as soon as possible.
